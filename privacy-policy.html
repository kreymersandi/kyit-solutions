Privacy Policy — Draft

KY IT Solutions respects the privacy of people who contact us. This draft describes the main ways information submitted through our website may be used and handled, with reference to Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).

1. Information collected

When you contact us, we may collect your name, email address, company, selected service and the enquiry details you choose to submit.

2. Purposes of use

We use enquiry information to respond to your request, consider your service needs, communicate with you and manage related business correspondence.

3. Third-party services

Our website is designed for GitHub Pages hosting and uses Formspree to process website contact form submissions. Information submitted may be processed by these service providers, including outside Singapore. We should confirm any current third-party handling arrangements before publication.

4. Protection and retention

We seek to take reasonable precautions appropriate to the nature of information provided and retain personal data only where necessary for business or legal purposes.

5. Requests and contact

For privacy-related questions, access or correction requests, or requests concerning consent, please contact admin@kyit.solutions. Requests will be assessed in accordance with applicable requirements.

6. Updates

This notice may be updated to reflect changes to our business practices, service providers or applicable law. A final effective date should be added on approval.
