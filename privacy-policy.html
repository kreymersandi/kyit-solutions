Privacy Policy

KY IT Solutions respects the privacy of our website visitors, prospective and existing clients, business partners, and other individuals who interact with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect and manage personal information through our website, business communications and the technology and consulting services we provide.

1. Information we collect

Depending on your relationship with KY IT Solutions, we may collect your name, business and contact details, company information, service enquiries, project requirements, correspondence, and information you provide while engaging our services. When you use our website, basic technical information may also be processed as needed to operate and secure it. Please do not send passwords or sensitive personal information through our general enquiry form.

2. How we use information

We use personal information to respond to enquiries, prepare proposals, provide and support agreed services, communicate with clients and business contacts, administer projects and relationships, protect our systems, and meet applicable contractual or legal obligations. Where required, we will seek appropriate permission before using contact information for unrelated marketing.

3. Protection, sharing and retention

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. Information may be shared with authorised personnel, providers supporting our operations, or other parties involved in delivering an agreed service, where appropriate, or when required by law. Depending on our business and service arrangements, information may be handled across jurisdictions with safeguards appropriate to applicable requirements. We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above or to meet legal and contractual obligations.

4. Client and project information

Information supplied to us as part of a client engagement may be subject to additional confidentiality, security, data-processing or contractual arrangements. Where those arrangements apply, we handle client information in accordance with the relevant agreement and applicable requirements.

5. Your choices and requests

Depending on the laws that apply, you may request access to or correction of your personal information, ask about its use, request deletion where available, or withdraw consent where processing depends on it. To raise a privacy concern or submit a request, email admin@kyit.solutions. We will review requests in accordance with applicable requirements.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our services, information-handling practices or applicable requirements. The date shown on this page identifies the latest published version.
