Privacy Policy

KY IT Solutions respects the privacy of our clients, prospective clients, partners and website visitors. This Privacy Policy explains how we may collect, use and protect personal information submitted through our website and through related business communications.

1. Information we may collect

We may collect information you choose to provide to us, including your name, email address, company or organisation, service interests, enquiry details and other information relevant to our communications or services.

2. How we may use your information

We may use personal information to respond to enquiries, communicate with you, assess service requirements, provide proposals or services, maintain business records, improve our website and support our business operations.

3. Protection and retention

We seek to use reasonable administrative, technical and organisational measures to protect personal information and retain it only for as long as reasonably necessary for legitimate business, operational or legal purposes.

4. Your choices and requests

Where applicable, you may request access to, correction of or updates to personal information you have provided to us, or ask questions about how it is handled, by contacting us at admin@kyit.solutions.

5. Sensitive information

Please do not submit passwords, payment card information or other highly sensitive personal data through the website contact form unless specifically requested through a secure and appropriate channel.

6. Updates to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our business practices, website features or applicable requirements. The latest version published on this website will apply.
